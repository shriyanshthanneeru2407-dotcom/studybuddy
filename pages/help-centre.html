Frequently Asked Questions

Need quick assistance? Browse our categorised product guides below.

How does StudyVision AI evaluate answers?
StudyVision AI scans handwritten documents to convert them into editable digital text. It calculates character accuracy confidence rates. The teacher always remains the final authority for grading and marks entry.
What happens if there's a Smart Timetable conflict?
If two classes or custom study blocks overlap on the same day and time, a warning alert will be displayed on the top of the timetable panel and dashboard view, highlighting the conflicting cards in red.
Is my chatbot conversation private?
Yes, all chat conversations with StudyBuddy AI are private and stored locally. Note that the chatbot is an educational assistant and should not be used as a medical therapist or doctor.
How do I order from the School Store?
Go to the School Store page, click "Add to Request" on the books or stationery items, review your request cart in the sidebar, and click "Submit Request". The school store administrators will be notified and approve your request for dispatch.
