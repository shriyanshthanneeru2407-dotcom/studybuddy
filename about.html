Our Mission

Designed for Classrooms. Built for Scale.

StudyBuddy is built by teachers, students, and technologists with a simple goal: to make academic administration efficient and personal learning intuitive, without unnecessary complexity.

A Modern School Companion

We started StudyBuddy because we saw classrooms struggling with disconnected apps. Attendance was on one spreadsheet, timetables on another, notices on a wall board, and digital grading somewhere else.

StudyBuddy pulls everything into a unified workspace. By organizing dashboards around user roles, we give students, teachers, and school leaders exactly what they need — and nothing they don't.

Our Values

  • Usability first: Accessible contrast, simple forms, readable tables.
  • Academic Focus: High utility, low distraction, clean layouts.
  • Human Centred AI: StudyVision AI processes documents, but teachers always grade.
