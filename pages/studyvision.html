Q1. Explain Newton's Second Law of Motion. Newton's Second Law of Motion states that the rate of change of momentum of a body is directly proportional to the applied force and takes place in the direction in which the force acts. F = ma, where F is force, m is mass of the object, and a is acceleration. momentum is mass multiplied by velocity.
